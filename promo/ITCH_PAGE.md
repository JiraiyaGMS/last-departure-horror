## Short description (the one-line tagline)

> You fell asleep in a bus station in 1987. The last bus leaves at 3:33 AM.
> The station has rules. Some of them are lies.

## Description

**Grayhollow Transit Center. November 3, 1987. 11:47 PM.**

You only meant to rest your eyes between coaches. Now the clock above the
gate says 11:47, the hall is empty but for fourteen passengers who will not
look at you, and the last service to the coast leaves Bay 9 at 3:33 AM.

You will need a valid ticket. Stub, stamp, and a punched transfer, from three
different rooms of a station that does not want you to leave. A night attendant
in a transit cap walks the halls with his keys. You will hear him before you
see him. When you look at him, he does not move.

The station has rules, posted on the wall. Break one and he knows exactly
where you are. But not all of them were written by the station.

Get caught, or miss the bus, and you wake on the same bench at 11:47 with
nothing in your pockets and everything you learned still in your notebook.

**Features**

- A first-person horror game that runs in your browser, on a desktop, a
  tablet or a phone, with keyboard and mouse, touch or a controller.
- A looping night, about eleven minutes long, that remembers what you learn,
  and a notebook that maps the station in pencil as you find your way round.
- A stalker who only moves when you are not looking, and who hears
  everything you do.
- Rules to obey, and rules to catch out.
- **Terror mode**, for anyone the game does not frighten: every time the
  lights blink he is closer, something breathes a pace behind you, the
  passengers move when you look away, and he knows where you hide.
- A PA console you work by hand, and, if you let it, your own voice on the
  station's speakers (it never leaves your computer).
- Fourteen passengers, each waiting for something they lost.
- Four endings, one of them hidden.
- Every sound made live in your browser, the echo of every room included.
- Difficulty settings, a brightness check, adjustable text, directional
  captions for the sounds that matter, reduced flashing and motion, and
  hints you can turn off.

**Best played with headphones, in the dark.**

## Controls

| Keyboard and mouse | Controller | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WASD, mouse | Left stick, right stick | Move, look |
| E | A | Use, pick up, hide, read |
| Tab | View | Notebook, with tonight's checklist |
| M | D-pad, in the notebook | The notebook's map |
| C | B | Crouch |
| Shift | RT | Run, against the rules |
| F / B | X / RB | Flashlight / fresh battery cell |
| G | LB | Throw a coin |
| R | Y | Show your ticket, or answer |
| Space / Q | LT / B | Hold your breath / leave, while hidden |
| Esc | Menu | Pause |

On a tablet or phone the controls are on screen. Play in landscape.

## Suggested page settings

- **Kind of project:** HTML.
- **Embed options:** viewport 1280 × 720, *Mobile friendly* on (landscape),
  *Fullscreen button* on. *Automatically start on page load* off: the game
  asks for a click to start, which is what lets it play sound.
- **Genre:** Horror. **Tags:** horror, first-person, psychological-horror,
  atmospheric, puzzle, time-loop, liminal-space, retro, mystery, short.
- **Content:** suggest a content warning for horror themes, death and
  sudden loud noises (Terror mode especially: screams and jump scares).
  The PA console can use the microphone if the player switches it on.
